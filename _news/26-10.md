**-** New preprint: [Training-Free Diffusion Planning with Analytical Local Scores](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01959). 
We propose a novel planner that distills analytical local scores from a diffusion model to generate smooth, collision-free trajectories for very large multiagent systems, in near real-time, and without training on demonstrations!
