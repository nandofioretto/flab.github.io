**-** Six papers from the RAISE Lab accepted at **[NeurIPS 2026](https://neurips.cc/)**! The work spans constrained generative models, safe code generation, robot planning, and multi-agent safety. Congratulations to our students and collaborators! :stars:

- [Search-Augmented Masked Diffusion Models for Constrained Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02727)
- [Constrained Code Generation with Discrete Diffusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16829)
- [Chance-constrained Flow Matching for High-Fidelity Constraint-aware Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25157)
- [Simulation-Informed Diffusion for Decentralized Multi-robot Motion Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27697)
- [Drift Flow Matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17244)
- [Colosseum: Auditing Collusion in Cooperative Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15198)

<br>

**-** New preprint: [SEABench: Benchmarking Endogenous Misalignment In Self-Evolving Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35596). We study how agent self-updates can improve task completion while introducing safety failures, even without adversarial intervention.
