Ferdinando (Nando) Fioretto is a Copenhaver Fellow and Associate Professor of Computer Science at the University of Virginia, where he leads the Responsible AI for Science and Engineering (RAISE) lab.  His research develops foundational machine learning and generative AI methods for scientific and engineering systems that must obey control objectives, hard constraints, safety requirements, and physical laws. In particular, his lab pioneered methods to enable the integration of constrained optimization within both predictive and generative AI models, resulting in applications in materials design, protein engineering, multi-robot motion planning, printed circuit board design, and power systems. Nando's research also focuses on the assurance of AI systems and multi-agent systems with particular emphasis on privacy, fairness, and safety as constraints along the entire data, model, and coordination pipeline.

He is a recipient of the Caspar Bowden PET award, the NSF CAREER award, the Google Research Scholar Award, the Amazon Research Award, the the ISSNAF Mario Gerla Young Investigator Award, the ACP Early Career Researcher Award, the AI*AI Best AI dissertation award, and several best paper awards. He has been a board member of the Artificial Intelligence Journal (AIJ) and an associate editor of the Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research (JAIR) and the INFORMS Journal on Computing. He his also a strong community building advocate, and co-organized various workshops, events, and summer schools at the intersection of ML and optimization, privacy, and safety.
